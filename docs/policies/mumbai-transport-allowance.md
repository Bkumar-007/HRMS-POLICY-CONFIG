# Acme Corp — Mumbai Transport Allowance

## Eligibility

- Mumbai-based employees in Grades **G1–G3** receive a monthly transport allowance of **INR 2,500**.
- Grade **G4+** in Mumbai may opt for cab reimbursement up to **INR 4,000** with receipts instead of the flat allowance.

## Claims

- Allowance is paid with payroll; no monthly claim form is required for the flat amount.
