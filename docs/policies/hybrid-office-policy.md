# Acme Corp — Hybrid Office Policy (Pune)

## Desk Booking

- Pune office desks must be booked via the portal for in-office days.
- Peak days (Tuesday–Thursday) require booking by **17:00 IST** the previous day.

## Attendance

- Employees tagged to Pune should be in-office at least **2 days per week** unless on approved WFH or leave.
