# Acme Corp — Maternity Leave Policy

## Entitlement

- Eligible employees receive **26 weeks** of paid maternity leave.
- At least **8 weeks** should be taken after delivery unless a doctor recommends otherwise.

## Notice

- Notify HR at least **8 weeks** before the expected due date when possible.
