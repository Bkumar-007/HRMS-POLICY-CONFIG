# Acme Corp — Travel Policy (v1.0)

Fictional sample policy for portfolio demos.

## Travel Advance

- Domestic travel advance limit: **INR 15,000** per trip for Grade G1–G3.
- Grade G4+ may request up to **INR 25,000** with manager approval.

## Booking

- Flights should be booked at least **7 days** in advance for domestic travel.
- Economy class is default for domestic flights.

## Expense Claims

- Submit expense claims within **15 days** of return.
- Original invoices (or digital copies) are required for claims above INR 500.
