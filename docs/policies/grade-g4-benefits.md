# Acme Corp — Grade G4 Benefits Addendum

## Travel

- G4 employees may book **business class** for international flights longer than **6 hours**.
- Domestic flights remain economy unless flight time exceeds **4 hours** and manager approves.

## Learning Budget

- Annual learning budget: **INR 50,000** for certifications and conferences.
