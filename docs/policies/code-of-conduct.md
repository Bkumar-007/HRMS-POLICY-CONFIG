# Acme Corp — Code of Conduct

## Respect

- Treat colleagues, customers, and vendors with respect. Harassment or discrimination is prohibited.

## Conflicts of Interest

- Disclose personal financial interests that could conflict with Acme business decisions within **7 days** of becoming aware.

## Confidentiality

- Do not share employee personal data, customer lists, or unreleased product information outside authorized channels.
