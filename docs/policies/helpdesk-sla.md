# Acme Corp — IT Helpdesk SLA

## Priority Levels

- **P1 (Critical):** production outage — response within **1 hour**, resolution target **4 hours**.
- **P2 (High):** major feature broken — response within **4 hours**, resolution target **1 business day**.
- **P3 (Normal):** how-to and minor issues — response within **1 business day**.

## How to Raise

- Use the HR assistant ticket flow (Phase 4) or email helpdesk@acme.example (fictional).
