# Acme Corp — Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Meal Limits

- Domestic per-diem meal cap: **INR 1,200** per day without receipts for short trips.
- Client entertainment requires prior manager approval above **INR 3,000**.

## Submission

- File expenses in the HR portal within **15 days** of the spend date.
- Claims older than **45 days** need HR_ADMIN exception approval.

## Non-reimbursable

- Alcohol (except approved client entertainment), personal travel upgrades, and fines are not reimbursable.
