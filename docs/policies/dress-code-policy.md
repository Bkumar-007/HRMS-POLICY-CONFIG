# Acme Corp — Dress Code Policy (v1.0)

Fictional sample policy for portfolio demos. Not affiliated with any real employer.

## Purpose

This policy sets expectations for professional appearance at Acme Corp offices, client sites, and company events.

## General Office Attire

- **Business casual** is the default for office days.
- Acceptable: collared shirts, blouses, sweaters, chinos, dress trousers, knee-length skirts/dresses, closed-toe shoes.
- Not acceptable: ripped clothing, beachwear, flip-flops, clothing with offensive slogans.

## Client Meeting Days

- When meeting external clients (in person or video), wear **business formal** or neat business casual.
- Prefer blazer or formal shirt; avoid loud patterns that distract on camera.

## Hybrid / Work-From-Home Video Calls

- Visible attire on camera should meet business-casual standards from the waist up.
- Company-branded apparel is encouraged on all-hands calls.

## Safety & Lab Areas

- Closed-toe shoes are mandatory in labs and facilities floors.
- Loose jewelry or dangling accessories must be secured where machinery is present.

## Religious & Cultural Attire

- Acme Corp respects religious and cultural dress. Reasonable accommodations are supported via HR.
- Safety requirements still apply in restricted areas.

## Enforcement

- Managers may give a private reminder for first-time issues.
- Repeated non-compliance may be escalated to HR under the Code of Conduct.

## Questions

- Ask your manager or email hr@acme.example (fictional).
- Policy questions can also be asked in the HR assistant chat.
