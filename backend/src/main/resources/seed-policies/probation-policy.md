# Acme Corp — Probation Policy

## Duration

- Standard probation is **6 months** from the joining date.
- Managers may recommend early confirmation after **3 months** for exceptional performance.

## Leave During Probation

- Casual leave during probation is capped at **1 day per month**.
- Earned leave is not available until probation ends successfully.
