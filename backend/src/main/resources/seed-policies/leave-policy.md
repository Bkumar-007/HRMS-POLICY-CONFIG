# Acme Corp — Leave Policy (v1.0)

Fictional sample policy for portfolio demos. Not affiliated with any real employer.

## Casual Leave

- Employees receive **12 casual leave (CL)** days per calendar year.
- CL may be taken for personal reasons with prior notice where possible.
- Maximum **3 consecutive** casual leave days without manager escalation.

## Sick Leave

- Employees receive **10 sick leave (SL)** days per year.
- Medical certificate required for sick leave longer than **2 consecutive** days.

## Earned Leave

- Employees receive **15 earned leave (EL)** days per year after probation.
- EL requests require **3 working days** notice unless approved as an exception by HR.

## Application Rules

- Leave cannot be applied for a date that is already a company holiday.
- Employees may only apply leave for themselves.
- Pending applications must be confirmed by the employee in the HR assistant before they are submitted to payroll.

## Blackout Periods

- During year-end closing (typically last 5 working days of March), EL requires HR_ADMIN approval.
