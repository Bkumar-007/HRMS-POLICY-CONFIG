# Acme Corp — Work From Home Policy (2026)

## Eligible Days

- Employees may work from home on **Fridays** with manager approval recorded in the HR portal.
- Up to **2 additional WFH days** per month are allowed for Grades G3 and above with 24 hours notice.
- Grade G1–G2 require manager approval for any non-Friday WFH day.

## Core Hours

- Core collaboration hours are **11:00–16:00 IST** on WFH days.
- Employees must be reachable on the company chat tool during core hours.

## Equipment

- Company laptop is mandatory for WFH; personal devices are not permitted for accessing payroll or HR systems.
